Unlike most of the other places in New Zealand, Milford Sound is a place that is best experienced in bad weather. The waterfalls only appear when it rains, and the more it rains, the more waterfalls there are. The fiord fills with a low mist that the boat moves through, and the scale of the cliffs — 1,200 metres from water to summit — becomes even harder to process.

Activity Details

Operator: Pure Milford
Cost: ~$276 NZD
Duration: ~12 hours

There are a few different ways to experience Milford Sound, but I would highly recommend doing a cruise to really take in the grandeur of the fiord. I booked an all-inclusive tour with Pure Milford, which included transport to and from Milford Sound, a cruise through the fiord, and lunch on board.
