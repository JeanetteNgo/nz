The Nevis Swing is a 134-metre pendulum swing suspended above a deep canyon in Queenstown. It’s the highest swing in New Zealand - and also one of the highest in the world.

I have a fear of heights, and falling from heights, so I am not sure why I keep signing myself up for these things. But as the wise saying goes, you only live once.

We booked tickets online on the AJ Hackett website . Since we've already done the Auckland Skywalk, we got a 20% discount - pretty good incentive to keep coming back for more.

On the day, just show up at the location on time, where you'll be weighed and prompted to sign a waiver form. Unfortunately, that was how I found out I've gained considerable weight since I first stepped foot in NZ ☹️

Once you've put on the harness and crossed the bridge, you will be on the platform where you're supposed to swing from. The operators will strap you in when it's your turn, ask whether you want to do a countdown or a surprise, and when you pick countdown, they will surprise you anyway.
