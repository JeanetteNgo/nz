Mount Fyffe

This was my first attempt ever to hike a mountain, so it's only right to place Mount Fyffe at the top of the list. While the hike is by no means technically challenging, it was relentlessly steep which made it a very physically taxing affair. I did not reach the summit due to the icy slopes past Mount Fyffe Hut, which my boots were not equipped for. Nonetheless, I had great views of the Kaikoura Ranges and the coast all the way up, which made for a very rewarding experience. I will definitely be back to conquer the summit one day!

Mount Isobel

This was my second attempt at hiking a mountain, and this time, I made it to the summit! I completed this hike at the tail end of winter, after heavy snowfall a week before, which allowed me to experience trudging through snow for the first time. Thankfully, this time I was armed with microspikes that I ordered from Taobao, which was a major confidence boost 😛 It was also a solo hike and drive, the first of many to come.

Sealy Tarns

This is simply put, stairmaster 101. And while I hate stairs when it comes to hiking, there was something special about doing it with my family in the drizzling rain, surrounded by the majestic peaks of Mount Cook National Park. We initially planned to do the full Mueller Hut route, but the impending storm that was brewing made us turn back. I'll be back for it someday though 🤪

Hooker Valley

This isn't so much a hike as a walk, and a very rewarding one at that. Much of the trail is flat and on a paved boardwalk, winding through the stunning landscapes of Mount Cook. You get to cross 3 swing bridges and see multiple alpine tarns for relatively little effort. A definite must-do for the 'gram!

Mount Sunday

If you're a fan of LOTR, look no further than Mount Sunday. Famous for being the filming location of Edoras, capital city of Rohan in the Lord of the Rings, this solitary hill offers unblocked paranomic views of the surrounding Canterbury Plains and the Southern Alps. The hike to the summit takes a mere 30-40 min, well worth it for the enormous payoff you get to enjoy at the top. Don't forget to bring along a packed lunch and some snacks!

Tongariro Alpine Crossing

Another must-do for LOTR fans - you get to hike through Mordor and see the famous Mount Doom up close! This is hands-down my favourite hike in NZ, due to the variety of terrain you get to experience which keeps the hike interesting. You do need to account for some planning and logistics, which can be made difficult by the unpredictable weather. Still well worth it, and I would love to attempt this in winter one day.

Smugglers Bay Loop Track

Located at Whangarei Heads, this short loop track offers a stunning walk through coastal forest, spectacular bays and a WW2 gun emplacement. Just beware of the merciless sun and heat if attempting to complete this in summer!
