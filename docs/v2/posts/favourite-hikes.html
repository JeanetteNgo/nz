A completely subjective and non-exhaustive list of the best hikes I completed in New Zealand, in no particular order.

Mount Fyffe

This was my first attempt ever to hike a mountain, so it's only right to place Mount Fyffe at the top of the list. While the hike is by no means technically challenging, it was extremely steep which made it a very physically taxing affair. I did not reach the summit due to the icy slopes past Mount Fyffe Hut, which my boots were not equipped for. Nonetheless, I had great views of the Kaikoura Ranges and the coast all the way up, which made for a very rewarding experience. I will definitely be back to conquer the summit one day!

Mount Isobel

This was my second attempt at hiking a mountain, and this time, I made it to the summit! I completed this hike at the tail end of winter, after heavy snowfall a week before, which allowed me to experience trudging through snow for the first time. Thankfully, this time I was armed with microspikes which I ordered from Taobao, which was a major confidence boost 😛 It was also a solo hike and drive, the first of many to come.

Sealy Tarns

This is simply put, stairmaster 101. And while I hate stairs when it comes to hiking, there was something special about doing it with my family in the drizzling rain, surrounded by the majestic peaks of Mount Cook National Park. We initially planned to do the full Mueller Hut route, but the impending storm that was brewing made us turn back. I'll be back for it someday though 🤪

Mount Sunday

Mount Sunday is famous for being the filming location of Edoras, capital city of Rohan, in the Lord of the Rings. Although Mount Sunday itself is really just a solitary hill, the views from the top are truly spectacular, offering panoramic vistas of the surrounding Canterbury Plains and the Southern Alps.

The hike to the summit is relatively short and accessible, making it a popular destination for tourists and fans of LOTR alike.

Tongariro Alpine Crossing

A staple photo for everyone who does the Tongariro Alpine Crossing. Set against the backdrop of volcanic craters and rugged terrain, the vibrant Emerald Lakes create a surreal and breathtaking landscape that is truly unforgettable.

Smuggler's Bay Loop Track

This is a hidden gem of a hike located in the Catlins, Southland. The track takes you through lush native bush and along rugged coastal cliffs, offering stunning views of the Pacific Ocean and the surrounding landscape. The highlight of the hike is definitely the secluded Smuggler's Bay, where you can take a refreshing dip in the crystal-clear waters or simply relax on the sandy beach. This is a must-do for anyone looking to escape the crowds and experience some of New Zealand's most beautiful coastal scenery.
