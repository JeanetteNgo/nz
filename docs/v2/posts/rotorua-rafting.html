Rafting down the Kaituna River is one of the most thrilling adventures you can experience in New Zealand. The river offers a perfect mix of gentle rapids and exciting challenges, making it suitable for both beginners and experienced rafters.

Activity Summary

Operator: Rotorua Rafting
Price: $63 NZD
Duration: ~2 hours

We boarded the raft with a lovely mother and son pair who are Chinese Australian, under the hands of a chill guide who kept us very safe, which I really appreciated! Some of the other rafts had more raucous crowds who seemed to push for more of a thrill ride and their guides were happy to oblige (spoiler alert: everyone went in the water a few times), so pick your crowd carefully if you are an anxious participant like me.

Before we set off, we were taught the brace position, which is crucial for the big drop at Tutea Falls. Basically, hold on tight to the paddle and ropes at the side, tuck in and heads down. We practiced it a bit, learned a Maori blessing chant and then it was action time!

During the course, we were guided through a few smaller drops to get us used to the motions and what to expect, which really helped to ease my nerves. Surprisingly, I found myself enjoying it and having fun, a far cry from my initial anxiety about the whole thing.
