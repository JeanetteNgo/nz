I've always wanted to try horse riding, and when I saw that there was a riding centre in Christchurch that offered beginner friendly treks, I knew I had to give it a go. We signed up for a 1 hour trek with Waimak River Riding Centre through BookMe for a scenic ride along the Waimakariri River.

Upon arrival, we were greeted by an overly enthusiatic dog who came up to us and sniffed my um, private areas, which was how I found out that this is actually a pretty normal thing for dogs to do??? We were then sat down for a quick briefing and to get fitted for our helmets, before being led to a round pen to meet our horses.

In the pen, the staff gave a demonstation on how to mount and dismount the horses, as well as how to hold the reins, speed up, slow down, stop or turn. We were then each assigned a horse according to our weight and build. My horse was named Diva, and all I can say is that she lived up to her name.
