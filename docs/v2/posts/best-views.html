A completely subjective and non-exhaustive list of the best views I captured in New Zealand.

1. Mount Sunday, Ashburton

Mount Sunday is famous for being the filming location of Edoras, capital city of Rohan, in the Lord of the Rings. Although Mount Sunday itself is really just a solitary hill, the views from the top are truly spectacular, offering panoramic vistas of the surrounding Canterbury Plains and the Southern Alps.

The hike to the summit is relatively short and accessible, making it a popular destination for tourists and fans of LOTR alike.

2. Cable Bay, Nelson

Cable Bay is a picturesque coastal area located near Nelson in the South Island, known for its stunning views of the Tasman Bay and the surrounding hills. This is a popular spot for watching the sunset, as the sun dips below the horizon and paints the sky with vibrant colors.

3. Emerald Lakes, Tongariro National Park

A staple photo for everyone who does the Tongariro Alpine Crossing. Set against the backdrop of volcanic craters and rugged terrain, the vibrant Emerald Lakes create a surreal and breathtaking landscape that is truly unforgettable.

4. Hokitika Gorge, Hokitika

The turquoise waters of the Hokitika Gorge are a sight to behold, surrounded by lush native bush and towering cliffs. Beware of the sandflies though, they are relentless and will absolutely ruin your photo-taking session with 0 mercy.

5. Haast River, Haast

The mighty Haast River winds through the rugged landscapes of the West Coast. This clearing can be accessed by following the Roaring Billy Falls track, and is a great spot to sit back and soak in the stunning views of the surrounding mountains and native forests.

6. Nugget Point, The Catlins

Nugget Point is an aptly named rocky headland located at the southeastern coast of the South Island. The surrounding area is a great place to spot wildlife, including seals, penguins, and seabirds, making it a must-visit for nature lovers.

Out of all the lighthouse viewpoints I've visited, this 100% takes the cake.

7. Cape Reinga, Northland

A solitary lighthouse stands at the northernmost tip of New Zealand, cementing itself as an iconic landmark against the endless expanse of the ocean. Cape Reinga is a place of great cultural significance to the Māori people, as it is believed to be the point where the spirits of the deceased depart for the afterlife.
