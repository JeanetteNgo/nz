The Auckland Skywalk is a thrilling activity that allows you to walk along the edge of the Sky Tower, one of Auckland's most iconic landmarks. The experience offers breathtaking views of the city and the surrounding harbor, making it a must-do for adventure seekers and those looking to see Auckland from a unique perspective.

Signing Up

Cost: $215 NZD
Duration: ~2 hours
