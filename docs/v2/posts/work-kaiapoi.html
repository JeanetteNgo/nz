Location: Kaiapoi
Period: 29 Sep - 19 Oct 2024
Work: Farming, Gardening

I spent 3 weeks working on a small family farm in Kaiapoi, a town located just north of Christchurch. As always, this was a work-for-accommodation arrangement which we found on HelpX.

I've always wanted to try working on a farm during my time here, and this wound up being my fourth and final work stint in New Zealand. It is also my favourite out of all of them, possibly because I finally get a break from staring at screens all day 🤠

Living & Meal Arrangements

We were provided our own separate unit in a duplex house, making this our most spacious living quarters to date. We had our own kitchen, toilet, 2 bedrooms, and a foyer. While the furnishings were a little sparse since we were the first guests hosted for work exchange, the basics were covered.

As usual, my brother and I scissors-paper-stoned to see who would get the nicer room. Unfortunately, I lost.

Thankfully, the pantry was well stocked with food - mostly breakfast items and snacks, and instant rice and noodles. Our hosts also brought us bread, eggs, minced meat and sausages from time to time. Unfortunately, I have an idea of where the mince and sausages we were eating came from 😭

Of course, being so close to Christchurch, we could top up our lunch and dinners with grocery and snack runs whenever necessary.

Week 1

Our first week was spent weeding strawberry beds, shovelling and laying wood chips, followed by laying down groundsheets, tilling of soil, and more weeding to prepare more beds. Unfortunately, we arrived and left before harvest time, so we did not get to taste any of the strawberries that were planted :(
