I spent 3 weeks working on a small family farm in Kaiapoi, a small town located just north of Christchurch. As always, this was a work-for-accommodation arrangement, which we found on HelpX. The farm was a 10-minute drive from the town center, and we were provided with a small room in the main house to stay in. The work was quite physical, but it was a great way to experience the rural lifestyle in New Zealand and to connect with the local community
