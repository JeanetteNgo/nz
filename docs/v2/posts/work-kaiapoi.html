Location: Kaiapoi
Timeframe: 29 Sep - 19 Oct 2024
Work: Farming, Gardening

I spent 3 weeks working on a small family farm in Kaiapoi, a town located just north of Christchurch. As always, this was a work-for-accommodation arrangement which we found on HelpX.

I've always wanted to try working on a farm during my time here, and this wound up being my fourth and final stint in New Zealand. It is also my favourite out of all of them, possibly because I finally get a break from staring at screens all day 🤠

Living & Meal Arrangements

We were provided our own separate unit in a duplex house, making this our most spacious living quarters to date. We had our own kitchen, toilet, 2 bedrooms, and a foyer. While the furnishings were a little sparse since we were the first guests hosted for work exchange, the basics were covered.

As usual, my brother and I scissors-paper-stoned to see who would get the nicer room. Unfortunately, I lost.

Thankfully, the pantry was well stocked with food - mostly breakfast items and snacks, and instant rice and noodles. Our hosts also brought us bread, eggs, minced meat and sausages from time to time. Unfortunately, I have an idea of where the mince and sausages we were eating came from 😭

Of course, being so close to Christchurch, we could top up our lunch and dinners with grocery and snack runs whenever necessary.

Week 1

Our first week was spent weeding strawberry beds, shovelling and laying wood chips, followed by laying down groundsheets, tilling of soil, and weeding of an entire field to prepare more strawberry beds. Unfortunately, we arrived and left before harvest time, so we did not get to taste any of the strawberries that were planted :(

As our work area was right by where the sheep and lambs frolicked, I of course took the chance to pet as many of them as I could 😈

Week 2

In our second week, we learned to sow seeds of various plants and flowers, and helped to lay out more ground sheets and stacked a ton of bricks which definitely felt like they... well, weigh a ton. We also weeded the green house and as usual, I petted all the lambikins I could :)

Week 3

All too quickly, our last week arrived. We planted sunflowers seedlings, laid more wood chips, and weeded what essentially felt like the entire plot of land. I am exaggerating of course (am I actually?), but this really put me off any possibility of owning a big plot of land anywhere, ever. The amount of work and upkeep required is really no joke.

A big thank you to Ben and Chloe, our gracious HelpX hosts who were willing to entertain us city people.
